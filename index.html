Important: For this simplified tool, current at or below the fuse rated current is shown as Continuous / ∞ and the melting-I²t equation is not applied. Above rated current, the transient estimate uses t = I²t / I². A real fuse does not follow a single I²t equation over its complete time-current range; use the manufacturer's time-current curve for final design verification. For a non-rectangular inrush pulse, calculate ∫i²(t)dt.
